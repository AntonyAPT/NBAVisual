---
title: Design Rationale
---

This visualization aims to show where some of the best NBA players shot from during the 2019-2020 season and how efficient they were. I chose to show a top down view of the basketball court as it shows the clearest view of all potential shooting points. For encodings, I chose dots to represent each individual shot because they offer a clear position while offering minimum clutter. Furthermore I chose the colors blue and orange for made/missed shots along with a beige background to offer high contrast and factor in for people with color blindness. For interactivity I chose a dropdown instead of a search bar because it allows people not familiar with the visual to know their options. A made/missed filter allows the user to reduce clutter and focus on what kind of shot they want to look at. The tool tip gives details on demand without cluttering the initial view. Initially, I thought about using a heatmap, but this would make showing individual shot information very difficult.

Data source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jonathangmwl/nba-shot-locations
